# CoverageFit v3.20.208 — CF-DISCOVERY-1.3

This forward release applies the add-to-calendar page's interaction principles to the secure opening and discovery payoff: one purpose, one dominant result, short confirmation copy, and calm card hierarchy.

## Visitor-facing refinement

- The secure opening uses the inherited CoverageFit logo, a short agency connection label, `Opening your Snapshot`, and one live status line.
- The carried-answer card says `You won’t have to start over` and gives a plain remaining-question count.
- Visible progress reports `4 quick questions left` instead of catalog position language.
- Completion says `Your first Snapshot is ready`, with `See my Snapshot` primary and `Add optional details first` secondary.

## Reference protection

The existing `/appointment/` add-to-calendar HTML, CSS, and JavaScript remain byte-for-byte unchanged and are protected by hashes in the UX clarity contract.

## Protected behavior

Discovery catalogs, carried-answer truthfulness, Snapshot-first completion, secure POST bootstrap, resume cookies, idempotency, confirmed identity reuse, anonymous entry, lead lifecycle, consent, SMS suppression, callback scheduling, Google Calendar integration, professional-role boundaries, Protection Score, and no-bind semantics remain unchanged.
